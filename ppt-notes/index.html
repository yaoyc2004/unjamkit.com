Private by design.

PPTX reading, matching, checking and export run in your browser tab. This page does not load analytics, ads or an AI service, and does not store deck content in localStorage or IndexedDB. A project file you choose to download contains extracted slide text, speaker notes, matches and statuses; keep it wherever you keep the presentation.

Text from images, charts, audio, animation and slide visuals is not compared. Speaker notes are extracted from standard PPTX notes placeholders. Unusual or password-protected files may need another workflow. Your browser may retain downloads and the host may log page requests; deck contents are not sent by this tool.

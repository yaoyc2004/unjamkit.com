    The details, in plain language.

    iPhones and many Android phones save photos as HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container). The files are small, but many Windows PCs, web forms and office systems will not open or accept them. This tool decodes each photo in your browser with libheif, then saves it as a standard JPG.

    Choose JPG to get one JPG per photo; several photos download together as a ZIP. Choose PDF to put every photo into one PDF, one photo per page, in the order shown in the list. Pages can match each photo or use A4 or US Letter with the photo fitted inside a small margin.

    Rotation saved in the HEIC file is applied, so photos appear upright. JPG output is 8-bit standard color: HDR brightness and transparency are not kept, and transparent areas become white.

    What does HEIC Convert do?

    HEIC Convert is a free HEIC to JPG and HEIC to PDF converter. Choose one or more HEIC or HEIF photos, then download JPG files (as a ZIP for several photos) or one combined PDF. Decoding and conversion happen in your browser; the photos are not uploaded.

    HEIC Convert questions, answered

    How do I convert HEIC to JPG?

    Choose your HEIC photos, keep JPG selected and click Convert to JPG. One photo downloads as a JPG; several download together as a ZIP of JPGs with the original file names.

    Can I convert several HEIC photos into one PDF?

    Yes. Choose the photos, select PDF and click Convert to PDF. Each photo becomes one page, in the order of the list. Choose A4 or US Letter if you plan to print the PDF.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. The decoder runs inside your browser and the JPG or PDF is created on your device. This page makes no network connections for your photos and does not load analytics. Downloaded files follow your browser and device settings.

    Does converting reduce quality?

    JPG is a lossy format, as is HEIC. At High (92%) quality the difference is rarely visible. Smaller quality or size settings make smaller files with less detail; check the preview before using them.

    Is location and camera metadata kept?

    No. Only the picture is converted. GPS location, camera details, dates and other metadata in the HEIC file are not copied into the JPG or PDF, which can be useful before sharing a photo.

    What about Live Photos, bursts and HDR?

    The still image of a Live Photo is converted; its motion is a separate video file and is not included. For a HEIC file holding several images, the primary image is converted. HDR photos are converted to standard color.

    Can I mix HEIC with JPG or PNG photos?

    Yes. JPG, PNG and WebP images can be added to the same list, which is useful for making one PDF from mixed photos. On an iPhone, Safari may hand over photos already converted to JPG; they are accepted the same way.

    Why does my iPhone sometimes send JPG instead?

    iPhone can convert automatically when you share or transfer photos (Settings → Photos → Transfer to Mac or PC → Automatic), or shoot JPG directly (Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible). This tool helps when you already have HEIC files on a computer.

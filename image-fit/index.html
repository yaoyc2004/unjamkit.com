The details, in plain language.

Enter the maximum allowed size. “200 KB” means the downloaded JPEG will be at most 200 × 1,024 bytes; it need not be exactly that size. If a form uses decimal KB, choose a slightly lower limit.

The tool adjusts JPEG quality and, if you allow it, image dimensions. A width setting is a maximum, not an instruction to enlarge the original. Tiny targets cannot preserve every detail.

For a PDF, the limit applies to the whole file. Each page is rendered at the resolution you choose (150 DPI is a good default for documents; 100 DPI or less for tight limits), saved as a JPEG and placed in a new PDF with the original page sizes. If the file is still too big, the tool lowers JPEG quality and then, if you allow it, the resolution. Text, links, forms and bookmarks do not survive; keep your original.

JPEG output does not preserve the source’s transparency, animation or original metadata. This version handles one image or PDF at a time. For iPhone HEIC photos, first use HEIC Convert to make a JPG.

What does Image Fit do?

Image Fit is a free image compressor with a target file-size setting. Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP, enter a maximum in KB and download a JPEG. Processing happens in your browser; the image is not uploaded.

How to compress an image to your file-size limit

  1. Choose your image.

    Select one JPG, PNG or WebP from your device. The input limit is 20 MB and 40 megapixels.

  2. Enter the file-size limit.

    Type 200 for a 200 KB target, 100 for 100 KB, or another limit. Add a maximum width if required. Leave Allow smaller dimensions on when reducing pixel dimensions is acceptable.

  3. Compress, inspect and download.

    Click Make it fit. Check the reported size, dimensions and image preview. Download JPG when the result is ready, then check that your form accepts it.

When this tool helps

An application form rejects your photo

Enter the form’s maximum file size instead of guessing JPEG quality. If the form also specifies pixel dimensions, set a maximum width and inspect the final size. This tool does not crop or validate passport-photo rules.

An email attachment or web image is too large

Use a smaller KB target for one photo or screenshot. Lower limits can blur text or fine details. Keep your original, and inspect the fitted image before sending it.

Image Fit questions, answered

How do I compress an image to 200 KB or 100 KB?

Choose the image, enter 200 or 100 in Maximum file size (KB), leave dimension reduction enabled if suitable, and click Make it fit. The tool adjusts JPEG quality and may reduce dimensions. Inspect the reported output size before downloading.

Does 200 KB mean exactly 200 KB?

No. It is a maximum target of 200 × 1,024 bytes, not an exact size. If a form uses decimal KB, choose a slightly lower target, such as 195 KB for a 200,000-byte limit.

Can I reduce a PNG or WebP below the limit?

Yes, supported PNG and WebP images are converted to JPEG. The output is JPG; transparent areas become white. It does not create a smaller PNG or preserve transparency.

Can I compress without losing quality?

JPEG compression is lossy. The tool searches quality settings and can reduce dimensions to meet the target. Very small targets may lose detail or be impossible under your chosen settings; check the preview and any error message.

Can I reduce a PDF to a KB limit?

Yes. Choose the PDF, enter the maximum file size and a resolution in DPI, and click Make it fit. Every page is turned into an image at that resolution, so the result looks like a scan: text is no longer selectable, and links and form fields are removed. Up to 100 pages and 50 MB.

Are HEIC, animated images or batch compression supported?

This version accepts one JPG, PNG or WebP image at a time. It does not read HEIC directly: convert iPhone photos with HEIC to JPG first. Batch processing is not supported, and JPEG output does not preserve animation or original metadata.

Is my photo sent to a server?

No. The browser reads the selected image locally and creates the JPEG on your device. UnjamKit does not upload the image or store a copy. Downloaded files remain subject to your browser and device settings.

Your content stays in this tab.

We don’t upload your files, pasted text, lists, QR content or Wi-Fi passwords. We don’t save them to a server, localStorage or IndexedDB. Clear the tool to discard its working state.

No account-shaped catch.

No login, email request or subscription. Tool libraries are served with the site. Processing limits protect your device’s memory; they aren’t paid upgrades.

Basic visit statistics. UnjamKit uses Google Analytics to understand page visits, traffic sources and device/browser types. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies, configured here to expire after 90 days. Enhanced measurement and advertising personalization are disabled. We do not send your files, filenames, pasted text, lists, QR content or Wi-Fi passwords. Page URLs sent by our tag exclude query strings and fragments; referrers are reduced to their origin. The boundaries: your browser may keep history, downloads or form state according to its settings. The website’s hosting provider may keep standard request logs.
